About

I study how a robot decides where to go next when its map is incomplete — which is always. My work sits between perception and planning: predicting the parts of a scene the sensors have not reached yet, keeping an honest measure of how uncertain that prediction is, and letting the uncertainty drive exploration instead of hiding it.

In practice that means training navigation policies in simulation, representing rooms and objects as 3D scene graphs, and measuring whether any of it survives contact with a real platform.

What I do

Train models that make decisions. Generative and policy models in PyTorch, from data pipeline to training loop to ablation — including the ones that have to run inside a control loop rather than a notebook.

Put them on a robot. Perception, mapping and navigation glued together with ROS 2 and Docker; simulation for volume, hardware for the truth. Latency and sensor noise are part of the problem, not an afterthought.

Measure it honestly. Benchmarks, baselines and calibrated uncertainty — the habit of publishing means I can say what a system does not do, which is usually the number a team needs.

Research interests

Selected work

ICCAS 2026 · First author

Latent flow matching for uncertainty-aware 3D scene graph completion

A generative model that completes a 3D scene graph from partial robot observations and reports how confident each completed node and edge is — so a planner can separate structure worth committing to from structure worth verifying.

IJCAS 2026 · First author

Turning explicit map prediction into uncertainty exploration

Instead of using a predicted occupancy map as if it were ground truth, the agent explores where the prediction disagrees with itself — converting model doubt into a navigation objective for object-goal navigation.

IJCAS 2025 · Second author

Attention-guided semantic navigation from pose, text and vision

Three modalities — where the robot is, what it was asked for, what it can see — fused by cross-attention and trained with an exponential reward that keeps credit assignment sharp near the goal.
